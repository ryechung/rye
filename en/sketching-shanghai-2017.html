ARCHIVAL RECORD · A-2017-SH-001

Sketching Shanghai: June 2017 Origins & Primary Evidence

Contemporaneous documentation, on-location fieldwork records, and open-air artistic practice initiated in Shanghai by Rye Liang (麦子).

Archival Overview

Sketching Shanghai (速写上海) was established in June 2017 in Shanghai by artist and curator Rye Liang (梁忠 / 麦子). Conceived as an open, non-institutional civic art initiative, it mobilized public participants to document the city's evolving urban vernacular, historical lilong alleys, and everyday street life through direct on-location drawing.

Chronology of Practice

  1. June 2017
    Inception & Public Calls: First public calls, open-air sketching gatherings, and contemporaneous drawings initiated along historical streets in Shanghai, establishing continuous public records.
  2. 2018—2021
    From Field Drawing to Civic Engagement: Evolution of on-location drawing into community-based art, interactive public workshops, and collaborative urban memory projects.
  3. 2022—Present
    Interdisciplinary Expansion: Deepening into human anatomical science and art, dual-city (Taipei—Shanghai) cultural comparisons, and archival literature/fiction.

Immutable Preservation & Cross-Verification

To prevent historical misattribution and maintain the integrity of public records, the primary materials from June 2017—including handwritten date stamps, event pages, photographic evidence, and original sketches—are permanently archived with immutable cryptographic timestamps.
