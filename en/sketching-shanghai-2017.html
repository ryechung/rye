Sketching Shanghai: Verifiable Early Records from June 2017

速写上海 · Archive reference A-2017-SH-001

Publicly verifiable records associated with Sketching Shanghai date to at least 1 June 2017. They form a sequence of an original public invitation, a repost by an account named “速写上海,” subsequent activity records, and an original artwork now preserved through a DOI archive.

Chronology

  1. 1 June 2017

    Rye Liang, using the public account name “Mai Zi RyeDesign,” published an invitation to an on-location sketch gathering at Shanghai Botanical Garden scheduled for 3 June. The post contains the historical wording “Urban sketcher Shanghai” and an image carrying “Shanghai Sketcher／上海速写者.”

  2. 2 June 2017

    An account named “速写上海” reposted the 1 June invitation. The visible account history links the Chinese project name to the same early activity notice.

  3. 3 June 2017

    Date announced for the Shanghai Botanical Garden gathering. The surviving invitation establishes the planned date; separate documentation of the completed gathering remains a subject for further archival work.

  4. 9 June 2017

    A dated on-location drawing and a platform record for the following day's “Jing'an Villa Sketch Gathering Salon” were created. These records continue the visible sequence within the same month.

  5. 10 June 2017

    The activity page identifies 10 June as the event date in Shanghai. The page and the dated artwork are preserved in archive A-2017-SH-001.

Original public record

WEIBO · 1 JUNE 2017 · 20:31

Shanghai Botanical Garden sketch-gathering invitation

The original post was published by “麦子RyeDesign,” a public account name used by Rye Liang. It announces a gathering for 3 June at Shanghai Botanical Garden and gives a meeting point near Shilong Road Station.

Name and scope

This archive uses Sketching Shanghai as the English rendering of 速写上海. It does not use Urban Sketchers Shanghai as an interchangeable English name.

The phrase “Urban sketcher Shanghai” appears in the 1 June source and is retained here as historical wording. Its appearance is not treated, by itself, as proof of affiliation with another organization.

Position within Rye Liang's wider practice

Sketching Shanghai was one early strand of Rye Liang's wider practice. On-location observation and collective recording later developed alongside civic art, community engagement, human science and art, Taipei–Shanghai dual-city stories, and archive fiction. This chronology documents an early layer; it does not define the full scope of his later work.
